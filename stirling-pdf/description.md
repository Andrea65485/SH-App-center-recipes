Stirling-PDF is a toolbox for PDF files that opens in your browser: merge, split, rotate, compress, convert to and
from other formats, add or remove pages, and read the text in scanned pages (OCR). It runs on your own machine, so
your documents never leave home. Everyone signs in: you choose the admin password when you install it. Its usage
reports and update pop-up are switched off. Licence: mostly MIT, but the image includes parts under the Stirling PDF
User License (free only for trial, evaluation or minimal use), so it isn't an open-source licence as a whole.
