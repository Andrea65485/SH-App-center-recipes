Piper speaks Home Assistant's answers out loud — the voice of a voice assistant — entirely on your own machine.
Home Assistant connects to it over the Wyoming protocol. It needs no account and keeps nothing private: only its
voice, downloaded once. This recipe gives it the British English voice Cori, whose recordings are public domain;
other voices exist, each under its own terms. Licences: wyoming-piper is MIT, the Piper engine inside it
GPL-3.0-or-later.
